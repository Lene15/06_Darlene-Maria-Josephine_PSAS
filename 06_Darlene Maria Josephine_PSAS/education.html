Description

Atomic Habits explains how small daily habits can lead to remarkable long-term results. Rich Dad Poor Dad

Rp 235.000

Description

Rich Dad Poor Dad is a personal finance and self-improvement book that compares the different mindsets about money taught by two father figures in the author’s life: his “poor dad,” who valued traditional education and job security, and his “rich dad,” who focused on financial education, investing, and building assets. Through simple stories and practical lessons, the book teaches readers the importance of financial literacy, passive income, entrepreneurship, and making money work for you instead of working only for money. It is widely known for changing the way people think about wealth and financial success.

Thinking, Fast and Slow

Rp 280.000

Description

Thinking, Fast and Slow explores how the human mind works and why people often make irrational decisions without realizing it. Nobel Prize-winning psychologist Daniel Kahneman explains that our thinking operates through two systems: “System 1,” which is fast, automatic, and emotional, and “System 2,” which is slower, logical, and analytical. Through fascinating examples, experiments, and real-life situations, the book reveals how biases, intuition, memory, and emotions influence judgment and decision-making in everyday life. It is widely regarded as one of the most influential books on psychology and behavioral economics.

The Psychology of Money

Rp 245.000

Description

The Psychology of Money explains how people think, behave, and make decisions about money. Instead of focusing only on technical financial knowledge, Morgan Housel shows that emotions, habits, and personal experiences often play a bigger role in financial success. Through short stories and real-life examples, the book discusses topics such as saving, investing, risk, greed, and happiness. Written in a simple and engaging style, the book teaches readers that managing money is not just about intelligence, but also about behavior, patience, and long-term thinking.
