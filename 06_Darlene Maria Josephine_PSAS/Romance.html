Pride and Prejudice

Rp 185.000

Description

Pride and Prejudice is a classic romance novel that follows Elizabeth Bennet, an intelligent and independent young woman living in early 19th-century England. When she meets the wealthy and reserved Mr. Darcy, misunderstandings and first impressions create tension between them. As the story progresses, both characters learn about love, pride, social expectations, and personal growth. Known for its witty dialogue, memorable characters, and timeless romance, the novel remains one of the most famous love stories ever written.

Me Before You

Rp 265.000

Description

Me Before You tells the emotional story of Louisa Clark, a cheerful young woman who becomes a caregiver for Will Traynor, a wealthy man left paralyzed after an accident. At first, the two struggle to understand each other because of their very different personalities and views on life. However, as they spend more time together, they develop a deep emotional connection that changes both of their lives. The novel explores love, hope, personal choice, and the impact people can have on one another.

To All the Boys I've Loved Before

Rp 175.000

Description

To All the Boys I’ve Loved Before follows Lara Jean Covey, a shy high school girl who writes secret love letters to every boy she has ever liked. She never intends to send them, but her life suddenly changes when the letters are mysteriously mailed out. As old crushes confront her, Lara Jean becomes involved in a fake relationship that slowly starts to feel real. The novel is sweet, funny, and heartwarming, making it one of the most popular teen romance books of recent years.

What Comes After Love

Rp 200.000

Description

What Comes After Love is a Korean-Japanese romance novel about Aoi, a Japanese woman, and Hong, a Korean man, who meet while studying abroad in Tokyo and fall deeply in love. Despite their strong feelings, cultural differences, misunderstandings, and personal struggles slowly pull them apart. Years later, fate gives them another chance to confront their past emotions and unresolved memories. Written from both Japanese and Korean perspectives, the novel beautifully explores love, distance, longing, and emotional healing with a calm and melancholic atmosphere.

See You Again Love

Rp 130.000

Description

See You Again Love is an Indonesian romance novel that tells the story of love, separation, and emotional reunion. The novel follows characters who struggle with unresolved feelings, painful memories, and the hope of meeting someone important again after being apart for a long time. Through emotional dialogue and relatable situations, the story explores themes of heartbreak, healing, friendship, and second chances in love. Its soft emotional tone and modern romance style make it popular among teenage readers.
