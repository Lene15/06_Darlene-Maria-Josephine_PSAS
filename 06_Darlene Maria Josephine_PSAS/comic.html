One Piece

Rp 210.000

Description

One Piece follows Monkey D. Luffy, a cheerful and ambitious pirate who dreams of becoming the King of the Pirates by finding the legendary treasure known as the “One Piece.” After gaining the ability to stretch his body like rubber from a mysterious Devil Fruit, Luffy sets sail across the dangerous Grand Line and forms a crew called the Straw Hat Pirates. Along their journey, they face powerful enemies, uncover hidden secrets of the world, and build strong friendships. The series is known for its exciting adventures, emotional backstories, humor, and incredible world-building, making it one of the most popular manga series of all time.

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba

Rp 210.000

Description

Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba tells the story of Tanjiro Kamado, a kind-hearted boy whose family is attacked and killed by demons. His younger sister, Nezuko, survives but is transformed into a demon herself. Determined to save her and avenge his family, Tanjiro joins the Demon Slayer Corps, a group of skilled fighters dedicated to protecting humanity from demons. Along his journey, he trains to become stronger, meets loyal friends, and faces powerful enemies in intense battles. The series is loved for its emotional story, beautiful artwork, exciting sword fights, and strong themes of family and perseverance.

Spy x Family

Rp 190.000

Description

Spy x Family follows a master spy known as Twilight, who must create a fake family to complete an important mission and maintain peace between two rival nations. Under the identity of Loid Forger, he adopts a young girl named Anya and marries Yor, an ordinary-looking woman. However, Loid does not know that Anya is secretly a telepath who can read minds, while Yor is actually a professional assassin. As they try to act like a normal family, misunderstandings, funny situations, and emotional moments constantly happen. The series combines action, comedy, and wholesome family interactions, making it both entertaining and heartwarming.

Batman: The Killing Joke

Rp 350.000

Description

Batman: The Killing Joke is a dark and psychological graphic novel that explores the intense rivalry between Batman and the Joker. The story focuses on the Joker’s attempt to prove that one terrible day is enough to drive any person insane. While Batman tries to stop him, the novel also reveals a possible origin story of the Joker, showing his tragic past before becoming Gotham City’s most dangerous criminal. Known for its deep themes, emotional tension, and iconic artwork, the graphic novel is considered one of the most influential Batman stories ever created.

Death Note

Rp 260.000

Description

Death Note follows Light Yagami, a brilliant high school student who discovers a mysterious notebook called the Death Note. The notebook has a deadly power: anyone whose name is written in it will die. Believing he can create a better world without criminals, Light secretly begins using the notebook as a vigilante known as “Kira.” However, his actions attract the attention of L, a genius detective determined to uncover Kira’s identity. The series becomes an intense psychological battle filled with mind games, suspense, and moral conflict, making it one of the most iconic thriller manga ever created.
