What Comes After Love

Rp 200.000

Description

What Comes After Love is a Korean-Japanese romance novel about Aoi, a Japanese woman, and Hong, a Korean man, who meet while studying abroad in Tokyo and fall deeply in love. Despite their strong feelings, cultural differences, misunderstandings, and personal struggles slowly pull them apart. Years later, fate gives them another chance to confront their past emotions and unresolved memories. Written from both Japanese and Korean perspectives, the novel beautifully explores love, distance, longing, and emotional healing with a calm and melancholic atmosphere.

Death Note

Rp 260.000

Description

Death Note follows Light Yagami, a brilliant high school student who discovers a mysterious notebook called the Death Note. The notebook has a deadly power: anyone whose name is written in it will die. Believing he can create a better world without criminals, Light secretly begins using the notebook as a vigilante known as “Kira.” However, his actions attract the attention of L, a genius detective determined to uncover Kira’s identity. The series becomes an intense psychological battle filled with mind games, suspense, and moral conflict, making it one of the most iconic thriller manga ever created.

Clean Code

Rp 430.000

Description

Clean Code teaches programmers how to write code that is simple, readable, organized, and easy to maintain. Robert C. Martin, also known as “Uncle Bob,” explains common mistakes in programming and demonstrates how clean coding practices can improve software quality and teamwork. Using practical examples from real code, the book covers topics such as naming variables, structuring functions, debugging, and writing efficient programs. It is considered one of the most influential books for software developers and beginner programmers alike.

The Diary of a Young Girl

Rp 235.000

Description

The Diary of a Young Girl is the real diary of Anne Frank, a Jewish teenager who hid with her family during World War II in Nazi-occupied Amsterdam. Through her honest and emotional writing, Anne shares her thoughts, fears, dreams, and daily experiences while living in hiding. The book gives readers a powerful and personal view of war, discrimination, hope, and human resilience, making it one of the most famous historical books ever written.

Batman: The Killing Joke

Rp 350.000

Description

Batman: The Killing Joke is a dark and psychological graphic novel that explores the intense rivalry between Batman and the Joker. The story focuses on the Joker’s attempt to prove that one terrible day is enough to drive any person insane. While Batman tries to stop him, the novel also reveals a possible origin story of the Joker, showing his tragic past before becoming Gotham City’s most dangerous criminal. Known for its deep themes, emotional tension, and iconic artwork, the graphic novel is considered one of the most influential Batman stories ever created.
