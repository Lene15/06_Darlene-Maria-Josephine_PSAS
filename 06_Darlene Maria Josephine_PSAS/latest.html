Description

Spy x Family follows a master spy known as Twilight, who must create a fake family to complete an important mission and maintain peace between two rival nations. Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans

Rp 405.000

Description

Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans explains the development, strengths, and limitations of artificial intelligence in a clear and beginner-friendly way. Melanie Mitchell explores how AI systems work, what machine learning can and cannot do, and why human intelligence is still difficult to replicate. The book discusses topics such as neural networks, self-driving cars, language processing, and the future of AI while also examining ethical and philosophical questions about technology. It is an excellent introduction for readers who want to understand AI without needing advanced technical knowledge.

The Midnight Library follows Nora Seed, a woman who feels disappointed with her life and regrets many of the choices she has made. Filosofi Teras

Rp 100.000

Description

Filosofi Teras is a self-improvement and philosophy book that introduces Stoicism in a simple and relatable way for modern readers. Through everyday examples, Henry Manampiring explains how Stoic philosophy can help people manage emotions, reduce overthinking, handle stress, and focus on things they can control. The book combines practical advice, humor, and real-life situations, making philosophy easier to understand and apply in daily life. It is especially popular among young readers because of its light writing style and useful lessons about mental resilience and self-control.
