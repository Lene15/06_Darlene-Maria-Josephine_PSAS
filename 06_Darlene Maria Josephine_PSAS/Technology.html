Clean Code

Description

Clean Code teaches programmers how to write code that is simple, readable, organized, and easy to maintain. Robert C. Rp 500.000

Description

The Pragmatic Programmer is a classic programming book that focuses on the mindset and habits needed to become a better software developer. Instead of teaching only one programming language, the book explains practical techniques for problem-solving, writing flexible code, debugging, teamwork, and improving productivity. Through real-world advice and examples, the authors encourage programmers to think critically, continue learning, and build reliable software. It is widely recommended for both beginner and experienced developers.

Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans

Rp 405.000

Description

Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans explains the development, strengths, and limitations of artificial intelligence in a clear and beginner-friendly way. Melanie Mitchell explores how AI systems work, what machine learning can and cannot do, and why human intelligence is still difficult to replicate. The book discusses topics such as neural networks, self-driving cars, language processing, and the future of AI while also examining ethical and philosophical questions about technology. It is an excellent introduction for readers who want to understand AI without needing advanced technical knowledge.

Description

Life 3.0 discusses the future of artificial intelligence and how advanced technology could transform human civilization.
