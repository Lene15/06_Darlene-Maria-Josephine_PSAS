Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind

Rp 300.000

Description

Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind explores the history of human beings from the earliest prehistoric humans to modern society. Yuval Noah Harari explains how humans evolved, formed civilizations, created religions, economies, and political systems, and eventually became the dominant species on Earth. The book combines history, science, anthropology, and philosophy to discuss major revolutions in human history, including the Cognitive Revolution, Agricultural Revolution, and Scientific Revolution. Written in an engaging and thought-provoking style, the book encourages readers to rethink humanity, society, and the future of our world.

The Diary of a Young Girl

Rp 235.000

Description

The Diary of a Young Girl is the real diary of Anne Frank, a Jewish teenager who hid with her family during World War II in Nazi-occupied Amsterdam. Through her honest and emotional writing, Anne shares her thoughts, fears, dreams, and daily experiences while living in hiding. The book gives readers a powerful and personal view of war, discrimination, hope, and human resilience, making it one of the most famous historical books ever written.

Guns, Germs, and Steel

Rp 325.000

Description

Guns, Germs, and Steel examines why some civilizations became more powerful and technologically advanced than others throughout history. Jared Diamond argues that geography, environment, agriculture, and access to resources played a much larger role in shaping societies than differences in intelligence or culture. Combining history, biology, anthropology, and geography, the book explains how factors like disease, food production, and technology influenced the rise and fall of civilizations across the world.

The Book Thief

Rp 260.000

Description

The Book Thief is a historical fiction novel set during World War II in Nazi Germany. The story follows Liesel Meminger, a young girl who develops a love for books while living with her foster family during the war. Narrated by Death itself, the novel explores friendship, loss, fear, and the power of words in difficult times. With emotional storytelling and memorable characters, the book is widely praised for its unique writing style and heartbreaking yet beautiful story.
