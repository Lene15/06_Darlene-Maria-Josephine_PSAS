Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint, Vol. 1

Rp 390.000

Description

Omniscient Reader’s Viewpoint, Vol. 1 follows Kim Dokja, an ordinary office worker whose only hobby is reading a web novel called Three Ways to Survive the Apocalypse. After years of being the novel’s sole reader, the fictional world suddenly becomes reality. As disasters begin and deadly scenarios appear, Dokja realizes he is the only person who knows how the story will unfold. Using his knowledge of the novel, he must survive alongside powerful allies and dangerous enemies in a world filled with monsters, constellations, and life-or-death challenges. The story combines action, fantasy, mystery, and clever strategy, making it exciting and suspenseful to read.

The Hunger Games

Rp 220.000

Description

The Hunger Games is a dystopian novel set in the country of Panem, where the Capitol forces twelve districts to send one boy and one girl each year to participate in a deadly televised competition called the Hunger Games. The story follows Katniss Everdeen, a sixteen-year-old girl who volunteers to take her younger sister’s place in the games. Inside the arena, Katniss must fight to survive while facing dangerous opponents, harsh conditions, and the pressure of entertaining the audience. The novel is filled with action, suspense, emotional moments, and themes of survival, sacrifice, and rebellion.

Laut Bercerita

Rp 100.000

Description

Laut Bercerita tells the story of Biru Laut, a university student and activist during the political turmoil in Indonesia in the late 1990s. Together with his friends, he fights for justice and freedom while facing danger, fear, and government oppression. The novel is divided into two perspectives: Laut’s experience as an activist and the emotional struggles of his family after his disappearance. Through powerful storytelling, the book explores friendship, family bonds, loss, courage, and humanity, making it both heartbreaking and meaningful.

Description

Harry Potter and the Sorcerer’s Stone tells the story of Harry Potter, an orphaned boy who discovers on his eleventh birthday that he is actually a wizard. He is invited to study at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry, where he makes close friends, learns magic, and uncovers secrets about his mysterious past. The Midnight Library

Rp 250.000

Description

The Midnight Library follows Nora Seed, a woman who feels disappointed with her life and regrets many of the choices she has made. One night, she finds herself in a mysterious library located between life and death, where every book represents a different version of her life based on the decisions she could have taken. As Nora explores these alternate lives, she begins to understand the meaning of happiness, regret, and what truly makes life valuable. The novel combines fantasy, emotional storytelling, and self-reflection, making it both inspiring and thought-provoking.
